Engineering activity remains owner-authorized evidence inspection with no assertion that unresolved provider rights were granted. Do not label a dataset's external rights VERIFIED merely because the owner supplied it.

No independent upstream exchange sublicense or provider representation binding redistribution/AI rights to these exact artifacts was established. Seek provider confirmation of intended use and applicable upstream restrictions, rather than assuming exchange restrictions are waived. Conversely, absence of public proof is not a finding of infringement or grounds to invent additional payment obligations.
