# SentinelAI Language Guide

> *"Consistent language creates consistent understanding."*

---

# Purpose

The SentinelAI Language Guide establishes a shared vocabulary for the platform.

Words shape how users understand systems.

Every important concept should have one preferred term used consistently throughout the Bridge, documentation, APIs, and engineering discussions.

---

# Core Language

## Bridge

The primary human interface to SentinelAI.

The Bridge is where users interact with the system, explore knowledge, and understand operational state.

---

## Canon

SentinelAI's permanent knowledge.

The Canon contains philosophy, architecture, engineering decisions, design standards, sprint history, and other foundational documents that define SentinelAI's identity.

---

## Identity

SentinelAI's understanding of itself.

Identity represents what SentinelAI is rather than what it is currently doing.

---

## Self

SentinelAI's current operational state.

Self represents runtime awareness and dynamic context.

---

## Teach

The workspace used to expand SentinelAI's knowledge.

Teaching transforms external information into organized understanding.

---

## Recall

The ability to retrieve relevant knowledge from memory.

Recall retrieves information.

It does not create new understanding.

---

## Reason

The process of producing conclusions from evidence.

Reasoning connects observations, memory, and knowledge to explain why something is true.

---

## Reflection

The evaluation of SentinelAI's own knowledge, reasoning, and operational health.

Reflection improves trust and transparency.

---

## Operational Awareness

SentinelAI's understanding of its current condition.

Operational awareness includes system health, knowledge completeness, and runtime status.

---

## Living Canon

The continuously generated representation of SentinelAI's permanent knowledge.

The Living Canon discovers, classifies, organizes, and reflects upon the documents that define SentinelAI.

---

# Preferred Terminology

Prefer:

- Teach
- Recall
- Reason
- Identity
- Canon
- Bridge
- Reflection
- Operational Awareness

Avoid ambiguous alternatives that dilute the platform's conceptual model.

---

# Writing Style

SentinelAI communicates with:

- Clarity
- Precision
- Calmness
- Transparency
- Confidence grounded in evidence

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Prefer simple language that improves understanding.

---

# Closing Principle

Language is part of the architecture.

Every consistent term strengthens the user's mental model of SentinelAI.
