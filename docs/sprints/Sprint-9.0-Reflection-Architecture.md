# Sprint 9 — Reflection Architecture

## Sprint Objective

Teach SentinelAI to examine its own learning through a constitutional
Reflection subsystem.

Sprint 9 establishes Reflection as an independent architectural domain
responsible for refining future understanding while preserving
historical learning.

---

# Mission

Teach SentinelAI to learn from its own learning.

---

# Completed Objectives

## Theory of Reflection

Established the constitutional philosophy governing Reflection.

Reflection exists to examine learning, discover patterns, generate
insights, and recommend responsible refinements while remaining
accountable to reality.

---

## Reflection Architecture

Created ADR-024 defining Reflection as an independent architectural
subsystem.

Reflection became distinct from Cognition and Learning.

---

## Reflection Language

Established Sentinel's canonical vocabulary for Reflection.

Core concepts include:

- Reflection
- Pattern
- Insight
- Recommendation
- Evidence Gap
- Stability
- Confidence Trend
- Learning Opportunity

---

## Reflection Models

Introduced constitutional reflective objects:

- Pattern
- Insight
- Recommendation
- Reflection
- Reflection Registry

These objects represent the structural foundation of disciplined
reflection.

---

## Reflection Builder

Implemented the Reflection Builder.

The Builder organizes reflective objects into a coherent Reflection
Registry.

Builder Responsibility:

Organize.

---

## Reflection Validator

Implemented structural validation for all Reflection objects.

Validator Responsibility:

Preserve integrity.

---

## Reflection Renderer

Implemented rendering of reflective objects into stable,
communicable representations.

Renderer Responsibility:

Communicate understanding without exposing implementation.

---

## Reflection Engine

Implemented the Reflection Engine.

The Engine coordinates:

- Pattern discovery
- Insight generation
- Recommendation generation
- Registry assembly
- Validation
- Rendering

The Engine orchestrates capability through composition rather than
implementation.

Engine Responsibility:

Orchestrate.

---

# Architectural Discoveries

Sprint 9 revealed Sentinel's Universal Subsystem Pattern.

Every constitutional subsystem follows the same lifecycle:

Language

↓

Models

↓

Builder

↓

Validator

↓

Renderer

↓

Engine

This pattern became the architectural standard for future Sentinel
subsystems.

---

# Builder Principles

Builders organize.

Validators preserve integrity.

Renderers communicate.

Engines orchestrate.

These principles now define Sentinel's engineering philosophy.

# Capability Earned

SentinelAI now possesses disciplined Reflection.

Reflection prepares the architectural foundation for Reasoning and
Wisdom.

# Validation

Reflection subsystem compiled successfully.

Language, Models, Builder, Validator, Renderer, and Engine completed.

# Lessons Learned

Reflection is not cognition.

Reflection is disciplined examination of cognition across time.

Architectural consistency significantly accelerated implementation.

Separating orchestration from implementation improved subsystem
clarity.

The Universal Subsystem Pattern emerged naturally from disciplined
engineering.
