## Architectural Discoveries

Several important architectural discoveries emerged during
implementation.

Inference became a first-class constitutional object connecting
premises to conclusions.

Coherence proved to be more fundamental than conclusions.

Renderers preserve interpretability.

Validators preserve trust.

Engines orchestrate capability through disciplined delegation rather
than accumulated responsibility.

Evidence earns trust.

Reasoning was not implemented as a monolithic capability.

Each completed layer reduced uncertainty for the next.

depends upon disciplined delegation rather than accumulated

with constitutional architecture rather than algorithmic complexity.

disciplined engineering.
