# Sprint 7.8 — Bridge Operational Awareness

## Objective

Establish The Bridge as SentinelAI's operational awareness workspace while defining the operational language that connects architecture, interface, and philosophy.
---

# Completed

## Bridge Summary Service

Created a unified summary service responsible for assembling information from SentinelAI's foundational services.

The Bridge now presents:

- Core Principles
- Connections
- Reflection
- Operational Health

through a single orchestration layer.

---

## Operational Health

Introduced an operational health service capable of reporting the current condition of SentinelAI.

The Bridge now communicates:

- overall operational state
- monitored services
- warnings
- system coherence

without exposing implementation details.

---

## Connections

Extended the knowledge graph into an operator-facing Connections workspace.

Relationship discovery now summarizes:

- organized under
- classified as
- references
- extends
- implements

instead of exposing graph terminology.

---

## Language Refinement

Established the first version of SentinelAI's operator language.

Examples include:

Knowledge OS → Intelligence OS

Remember Knowledge → Refresh Knowledge

Knowledge Graph → Connections

Canonical Documents → Principle Documents

Canon Health → Core Principles

Canon Status → Identity Status

This separates engineering language from operator language while preserving architectural accuracy.

---

## Identity

Refined the Identity workspace to emphasize SentinelAI's foundational principles rather than internal implementation.

The Identity workspace now defines:

- principles
- architecture
- foundational knowledge

that shape SentinelAI.

---

# Architectural Outcome

Sprint 7.8 established The Bridge as SentinelAI's operational awareness workspace.

Responsibilities remain clearly separated:

Bridge observes.

Backend computes.

Services orchestrate.

The interface communicates.

---

# What SentinelAI Learned

Operational understanding should never expose implementation details.

Engineering language exists for builders.

Operator language exists for understanding.

The Bridge became the translation layer between the two.

---

# What Became Clear

Coherent software requires more than good architecture.

Architecture, language, and philosophy must reinforce one another.

This realization led to the Sentinel Triangle.

---

# Next Sprint

Sprint 7.9 will continue strengthening SentinelAI's operational experience while preserving the architectural boundaries established in Sprint 7.8.
