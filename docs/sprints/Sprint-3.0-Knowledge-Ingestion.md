
# Sprint 3.0 — Knowledge Ingestion

## Milestone

This sprint taught SentinelAI how to remember knowledge.

## Objective

Create a reliable knowledge ingestion pipeline.

## Major Deliverables

- Document upload
- Chunking
- Embeddings
- Fingerprinting
- Knowledge ingestion workflow

## Architecture Decisions

- Every document receives a fingerprint.
- Documents are chunked before embedding.
- Embeddings become the foundation of long-term memory.

## Lessons Learned

Knowledge quality begins with ingestion quality.
