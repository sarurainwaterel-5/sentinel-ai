Mission

Sprint 18 transformed SentinelAI's existing Reflection architecture into
a governed, longitudinal, historically accountable, and persistent
cognitive faculty.

Sprint 9.0 established the architectural concept of Reflection.

Sprint 18 operationalized that philosophy.

The primary objective was not to make Sentinel produce more conclusions.

The objective was to enable Sentinel to:

examine accumulated Learning Events,
refuse unsupported Reflection,
discover evidence-supported historical Patterns,
generate bounded Insights,
produce bounded Recommendations,
evaluate Reflection confidence,
remain constitutionally governed,
examine Understanding evolution across time,
preserve reflective uncertainty,
preserve historical cognitive outcomes,
and recover Reflection history across runtime boundaries.

The resulting capability can be summarized as:
I learned.
    ↓
I reflected.
    ↓
I governed the Reflection.
    ↓
I recorded what happened.
    ↓
I remembered it later.

Commander's Intent

Reflection must improve SentinelAI's understanding without granting
Reflection authority it has not earned.

Reflection therefore remains separated from:

Learning,
Reasoning,
constitutional authority,
persistence authority,
execution authority.

Historical Reflection must preserve both successful and unsuccessful
cognitive outcomes.

A weak, insufficient, conflicted, or constitutionally inadmissible
Reflection remains part of Sentinel's intellectual history.

History is not a highlight reel.

History is evidence.

Architectural Principle

The central Sprint 18 principle became:

Historical cognition may be examined, challenged, superseded, and
refined. It may not be silently rewritten.

This principle governs both Learning Event history and Reflection history.

Domain-Cohort Comparability

Sprint 18 introduced domain-cohort comparability.

The corrected rule became:

Reflection history is sufficiently comparable when at least one domain
is represented across two or more distinct Learning Events.

operate across diverse intellectual history.

Adding unrelated Learning Events must not invalidate an already
comparable historical cohort.

This prevents accumulated intellectual diversity from making Sentinel
progressively less capable of Reflection.

Temporal Learning Analysis

Sprint 18 introduced deterministic temporal cognition.

Temporal analysis may establish:

chronological order,

gaps between Learning Events.

Temporal analysis may not establish:

improvement,
decline,
correctness,
weakening,
strengthening,
revision,
contradiction.

The governing principle is:

Time establishes sequence. Time does not establish meaning.

Equal timestamps preserve caller-supplied ordering rather than allowing
an arbitrary identifier to masquerade as temporal evidence.

Understanding Evolution

Sprint 18 introduced bounded Understanding evolution analysis.

The faculty can classify supported changes as:
STABLE
STRENGTHENED
WEAKENED
REVISED
CONTRADICTED
UNRESOLVED

These classifications are evidence-bound.

Chronology alone does not imply change.

Different Understanding identifiers do not automatically imply

String difference alone does not prove contradiction.

Explicit structured support is required for contradiction authority.

Understanding Lineage

Explicit Understanding lineage was introduced to preserve intellectual
continuity across distinct Understanding states.

Example:
understanding-v1
      ↓
    revised
      ↓
understanding-v2
      ↓
  strengthened
      ↓
understanding-v3

Lineage is declared explicitly.

The system does not infer lineage from:

similar titles,
similar prose,
timestamps,
shared domains,
or identifier similarity.

The governing principle is:

Similarity is not lineage.

Lineage is acyclic.

Branching history may exist, but Sentinel will not silently select a
preferred branch and represent it as the authoritative lifecycle.

Lineage-Grounded Evolution

Sprint 18 separated declared cognitive history from observed cognitive
change.

Example:
DECLARED:
v1 → STRENGTHENED → v2

OBSERVED:
confidence decreased
evidence support contracted

ASSESSMENT:
observed = WEAKENED
declared = STRENGTHENED
supported = False
conflict = True

Neither the lineage nor the Understanding state is rewritten.

Both the declaration and observed evidence remain visible.

This establishes the principle:

Declared history is evidence to inspect, not authority to obey blindly.
