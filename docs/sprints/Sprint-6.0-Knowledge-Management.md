
# Sprint 6.0 — Knowledge Management

## Milestone

This sprint organized SentinelAI's knowledge.

## Objective

Create services responsible for remembering, organizing, and managing knowledge.

## Major Deliverables

- Knowledge Management Service
- Metadata improvements
- Knowledge organization
- Repository structure

## Architecture Decisions

- Knowledge management separated from retrieval.
- Single-responsibility services adopted.

## Lessons Learned

Knowledge should be curated, not simply stored.

## Next Sprint

Sprint 6.4 — Knowledge Analytics
