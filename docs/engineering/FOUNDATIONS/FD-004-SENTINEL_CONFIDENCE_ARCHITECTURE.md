# FD-004 — Sentinel Confidence Architecture

This foundation records the confidence rules already established in
[FD-001](FD-001-SENTINEL_ENGINEERING_CULTURE.md) and the
[cognitive doctrine](../../architecture/cognitive/COGNITIVE_ARCHITECTURE_DOCTRINE.md).

There is no universal Sentinel confidence score. Each faculty owns the question
its confidence answers, the evidence it uses, and the explanation it preserves.

Reasoning confidence concerns support for a conclusion. Planning confidence
concerns the viability and specification of a proposed course of action.
Verification confidence concerns the reliability and coverage of the examination.
Reflection confidence concerns support for an observation about recorded learning.

Confidence calculation belongs to an independent specialist. Engines and
formatters preserve the resulting score, level, basis, factors, and uncertainty.
They do not improve a judgment merely by communicating it confidently.

Confidence cannot replace missing evidence, unknown provenance, incomplete
coverage, or unresolved assumptions. High confidence that evidence is insufficient
is different from high confidence in a proposed conclusion.

Constitutional admissibility is a separate judgment. Confidence never grants
admissibility or execution authority. An unavailable constitutional assessment
must remain explicitly unassessed; a numerical fallback must not be presented as
a measured degree of compliance or conflict.

Platform readiness and credential configuration are observations, not confidence
scores. Independently evaluated semantic accuracy is an empirical result, not a
value inferred from scripted unit tests.
