# FD-005 — Sentinel Observable Cognition

This foundation restates [FD-001](FD-001-SENTINEL_ENGINEERING_CULTURE.md),
[Trust Through Explainability](../../design/COGNITIVE_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md),
and [ADR-034](../../architecture/decisions/ADR-034-Governed-Persistent-Reflection-History.md).

Sentinel is explainable through structured artifacts, not through private model
deliberation. Observe the operation's inputs, authoritative result, and boundaries.

Where applicable, expose evidence identifiers, source attribution, standards,
checks, coverage, dependencies, assumptions, risks, findings, conditions,
confidence factors, limitations, and bounded workflow stages. Communicated
artifacts must preserve the judgment supplied by their owning faculty.

A connection shows its source, target, relationship, and observed basis. Ambiguous
or missing references remain visible instead of acquiring invented targets.
An acquisition record describes evidence added to memory; it does not claim that
semantic understanding was established merely by storing a document.

Operational condition comes from actual dependency and schema observations.
Always distinguish available storage, configured credentials, provider
availability, evidence confidence, constitutional assessment, and research
acceptance. Failures must communicate what cannot currently be relied upon and
the conditions required for reconsideration without exposing credentials or raw
provider payloads.

Historical cognitive outcomes are append-only. Preserve insufficient, conflicted,
and inadmissible reflections alongside successful outcomes. Later observations
may challenge a result but may not rewrite the earlier record.

Observation does not grant remediation authority. Use the established sequence:
Detect → Diagnose → Propose → Approve → Repair/Rerun → Re-verify.
Human authorization remains independent from cognitive or constitutional judgment.
