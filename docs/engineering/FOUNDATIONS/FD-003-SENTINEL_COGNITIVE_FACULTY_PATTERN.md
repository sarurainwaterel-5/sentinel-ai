# FD-003 — Sentinel Cognitive Faculty Pattern

This foundation restates the accepted
[Cognitive Architecture Doctrine](../../architecture/cognitive/COGNITIVE_ARCHITECTURE_DOCTRINE.md)
and the [Constitutional Subsystem Pattern](../../architecture/SENTINEL_SUBSYSTEM_PATTERN.md).

## Shared grammar

Structured input → Normalize → Analyze → Evaluate → Structured result →
Coordinate → Communicate.

Language defines meaning. Models define structure. Builders organize. Validators
protect integrity. Renderers communicate. Engines coordinate capability.

## Independent responsibilities

Each specialist owns one responsibility. Evidence analysis does not become
confidence calculation; confidence does not become constitutional admissibility;
a formatter does not become a reasoning authority. Orchestrators sequence
specialists, transfer explicit contracts, and preserve their outputs.

Planning produces a proposed course of action. Verification independently inspects
a declared subject and scope. Reflection examines authoritative Learning Events.
Historical persistence follows completed cognition and governance rather than
influencing their judgments.

## Observable results

Expose source identifiers, structured artifacts, confidence basis, limitations,
missing evidence, bounded workflow stages, and recommended next steps. Do not
expose private model deliberation. A repeatable deterministic connection or pattern
is an observed structure, not proof of semantic truth or improvement.

## Fail closed without erasing history

Missing evidence, ambiguous provenance, unavailable judgment, and incomplete
coverage remain explicit. An unassessed constitutional result is not admissible.
Confidence in a result cannot compensate for absent constitutional evaluation.
Preserve rejected and insufficient outcomes as immutable historical records.

The ADR-037 proposition-to-inference boundary remains closed until its independent
semantic benchmark and architecture review satisfy the documented acceptance
criteria. Scripted verdict enforcement alone cannot open that boundary.

## Human authority

The public interface communicates what was observed and what remains uncertain.
It may request a bounded, operator-selected capability. It never converts a plan,
verification, reflection, or stored recommendation into automatic execution.
