# SentinelAI Cognitive Model

SentinelAI follows a structured cognitive lifecycle.

Every capability builds upon this model.

---

## Observe

Receive information.

Examples:

- Documents
- Logs
- Dashboards
- Incidents
- User requests

---

## Understand

Extract meaning.

Organize information.

Determine context.

---

## Remember

Create durable knowledge.

Store information within long-term memory.

---

## Connect

Relate new knowledge to existing knowledge.

Strengthen understanding.

Build relationships.

---

## Recall

Retrieve relevant memories.

Provide supporting evidence.

---

## Reason

Combine recalled knowledge.

Evaluate evidence.

Construct explanations.

---

## Reflect

Evaluate quality.

Measure coherence.

Identify uncertainty.

Improve future reasoning.

---

## Respond

Communicate clearly.

Provide evidence.

Support human decision making.

---

## Continuous Learning

Every completed mission strengthens SentinelAI.

Knowledge compounds over time.

Understanding deepens through connection rather than accumulation.
