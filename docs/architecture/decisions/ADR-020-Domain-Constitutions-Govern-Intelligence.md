Domain Constitutions Govern Intelligence Development

Status

Accepted

Context

Operational Domains originally represented organizational boundaries.

As SentinelAI evolved, it became clear that domains required governing principles before meaningful expertise could emerge.

Knowledge without governing principles creates inconsistency.

The architecture therefore required constitutional documents that define how each domain develops understanding.

Decision

Each Operational Domain shall own its own constitutional documentation.

Minimum structure:

DOMAIN.md

PRINCIPLES.md

EVIDENCE.md

KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md

ROADMAP.md

Additional documents may be introduced as domains mature.

Consequences

Operational Domains become independent reasoning contexts.

Knowledge becomes governed.

Evidence becomes accountable.

Reasoning becomes consistent.

Domain maturity becomes measurable.

Future contributors inherit constitutional guidance before implementation.

Architectural Principles

Reality precedes evidence.

Evidence precedes knowledge.

Knowledge precedes reasoning.

Reasoning precedes understanding.

Understanding precedes wisdom.

Long-Term Impact

Domain Constitutions become the mechanism through which SentinelAI develops expertise while maintaining coherence across every Operational Domain.
