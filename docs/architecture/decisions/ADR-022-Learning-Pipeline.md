# ADR-022 — Learning Pipeline

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Sprint 8.3A established SentinelAI's Cognitive Foundation.

SentinelAI possessed a canonical cognitive language, cognitive models,
registry, validator, and renderer.

However, SentinelAI still lacked a disciplined process for transforming
observations into understanding.

Without such a process, cognition remained static.

The platform therefore required a constitutional learning architecture
capable of producing explainable understanding while preserving complete
traceability to reality.

---

## Decision

SentinelAI adopts a Learning Pipeline.

The Learning Pipeline defines the canonical process by which
understanding is earned.

Learning shall proceed through the following cognitive stages.

Reality

↓

Observation

↓

Evidence

↓

Concept Discovery

↓

Principle Discovery

↓

Relationship Discovery

↓

Understanding Builder

↓

Learning Recorder

↓

Cognitive Registry

Each stage possesses a single responsibility.

No stage may perform the responsibilities of another.

---

## Observation

Observations record reality.

Observations do not interpret reality.

They preserve what has been observed.

---

## Evidence

Evidence anchors cognition to reality.

Every concept, principle, relationship, and understanding shall remain
traceable to supporting evidence.

SentinelAI shall distinguish evidence from interpretation.

---

## Concept Discovery

Concept discovery recognizes reusable ideas contained within
observations.

Concepts describe reality.

Concept discovery does not infer understanding.

---

## Principle Discovery

Principle discovery identifies enduring governing statements.

Principles govern understanding.

Principles remain separate from individual observations.

---

## Relationship Discovery

Relationship discovery organizes cognitive objects into meaningful
structure.

Relationships transform isolated cognitive objects into organized
knowledge.

Relationships organize understanding.

---

## Understanding Builder

Understanding is not extracted.

Understanding is built.

Understanding emerges through the disciplined organization of:

- observations
- evidence
- concepts
- principles
- relationships

Understanding remains accountable to reality.

---

## Learning Recorder

Learning records the evolution of understanding.

Learning answers:

"How did SentinelAI come to understand this?"

Learning preserves:

- cognitive changes
- supporting evidence
- affected domains
- timestamps
- summaries

Learning introduces temporal reasoning into cognition.

---

## Architectural Principles

The Learning Pipeline adopts the following constitutional principles.

Reality remains the only editable source of truth.

Observations record reality.

Evidence anchors reality.

Concepts recognize reality.

Principles govern understanding.

Relationships organize understanding.

Understanding is built.

Learning records the evolution of understanding.

Every cognitive layer must earn the next layer.

---

## Separation of Responsibilities

Operational Domains define expertise.

The Cognitive Engine applies expertise.

The Learning Pipeline organizes expertise.

Memory preserves expertise.

No subsystem shall assume responsibilities assigned to another.

---

## Consequences

SentinelAI now possesses an explainable learning architecture.

Future semantic extraction, language models, reasoning engines,
reflection systems, knowledge graphs, and autonomous agents shall
populate the Learning Pipeline rather than bypass it.

This preserves architectural coherence regardless of future
implementation technology.

---

## Capability Earned

Sprint 8.3B establishes SentinelAI's first complete cognitive learning
cycle.

SentinelAI now possesses the architectural capability to transform
observations into structured understanding through a transparent,
traceable, and explainable learning process.

The platform is now capable of earning understanding rather than merely
retrieving information.
