# ADR-027: Constitutional Deliberation Subsystem

## Status

Accepted

---

# Context

Sprint 10 completed SentinelAI's constitutional Reasoning subsystem.

Reasoning enables SentinelAI to organize, validate, and communicate
traceable reasoning while preserving accountability to evidence and
constitutional boundaries.

Reasoning, however, does not determine which responsible path should be
preferred when multiple coherent possibilities remain.

A new constitutional capability is required to evaluate responsible
alternatives without replacing human judgment.

This capability is Deliberation.

---

# Decision

SentinelAI shall introduce a constitutional Deliberation subsystem.

Deliberation evaluates responsible possibilities produced by
Reasoning.

Deliberation shall preserve constitutional principles, expose
tradeoffs, acknowledge uncertainty, evaluate constraints, and prepare
recommendations while preserving human agency.

Deliberation shall never replace human judgment.

The subsystem shall follow the Universal Subsystem Pattern.

# Architectural Responsibilities

Reasoning answers:

"What follows coherently from current understanding?"

Deliberation answers:

"Among the constitutionally acceptable possibilities, which option
should be preferred, and why?"

Reasoning constructs justified conclusions.

Deliberation evaluates responsible options.

The two subsystems remain independent while operating sequentially.

---

# Constitutional Responsibilities

The Deliberation subsystem shall:

- evaluate competing options
- preserve constitutional principles
- expose tradeoffs
- acknowledge uncertainty
- evaluate constraints
- assess risks
- assess benefits
- preserve reversibility
- preserve proportionality
- support human judgment

The subsystem shall never:

- invent evidence
- replace reasoning
- conceal tradeoffs
- conceal uncertainty
- replace human authority

---

# Core Constitutional Objects

The initial constitutional language shall include:

- Possibility
- Option
- Principle
- Value
- Constraint
- Tradeoff
- Risk Assessment
- Benefit Assessment
- Consequence
- Restraint Assessment
- Deliberative Recommendation
- Deliberation Report

These objects define the constitutional vocabulary required for
responsible deliberation.

---

# Architectural Consequences

Completion of the Deliberation subsystem establishes a constitutional
layer between Reasoning and human decision-making.

The resulting cognitive progression becomes:

Knowledge

↓

Understanding

↓

Reflection

↓

Reasoning

↓

Deliberation

↓

Human Judgment

↓

Action

↓

Reality

This progression preserves SentinelAI's commitment to accountability,
human agency, and disciplined decision support.

---

# Principles Validated

Reasoning precedes deliberation.

Evidence earns trust.

Trust preserves integrity.

Integrity preserves coherence.

Deliberation exposes responsible alternatives.

Human judgment remains sovereign.

The most responsible recommendation may be restraint.

Capability is cultivated through constitutional architecture.

---

# Architectural Outcome

Deliberation extends SentinelAI's constitutional architecture from
coherent reasoning toward responsible judgment.

The subsystem reinforces the Universal Subsystem Pattern as a
repeatable methodology for cultivating increasingly sophisticated
cognitive capabilities while preserving accountability to reality.
