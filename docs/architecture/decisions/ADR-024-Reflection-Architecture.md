# ADR-024 — Reflection Architecture

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Sprint 8 established SentinelAI's Cognitive Foundation, Learning
Pipeline, and Cognitive Memory.

SentinelAI became capable of representing understanding, earning
understanding, and preserving the evolution of understanding through
Learning Events.

However, historical learning alone does not improve future
understanding.

The platform therefore requires a Reflection Architecture capable of
examining cognitive history, identifying meaningful patterns, and
producing recommendations that improve future understanding while
remaining accountable to reality.

---

## Decision

SentinelAI adopts a Reflection Architecture.

Reflection examines Learning.

Reflection does not modify Learning.

Reflection produces Reflections.

Reflections inform future Learning.

Reflection therefore refines understanding without altering historical
memory.

---

## Reflection Cycle

Learning History

↓

Pattern Discovery

↓

Evaluation

↓

Insight

↓

Recommendations

↓

Future Learning

Reflection completes the constitutional intelligence cycle.

---

## Responsibilities

Reflection shall:

- examine Learning Events
- discover patterns
- identify recurring strengths
- identify recurring weaknesses
- identify contradictions
- identify insufficient evidence
- recommend future learning

Reflection shall not:

- modify historical Learning Events
- rewrite memory
- alter evidence
- replace understanding
- create observations

---

## Reflection Objects

Reflection introduces a new constitutional cognitive object.

Reflection

A Reflection represents a structured evaluation of accumulated
Learning Events.

A Reflection preserves:

- observations examined
- learning examined
- patterns discovered
- insights generated
- recommendations produced

Reflections remain traceable to Learning Events.

---

## Architectural Principles

Reflection examines understanding.

Reflection seeks patterns rather than isolated events.

Reflection remains accountable to evidence.

Reflection preserves intellectual humility.

Reflection welcomes revision.

Reflection improves future understanding.

Reflection shall never replace reality.

---

## Separation of Responsibilities

Learning records understanding.

Memory preserves understanding.

Reflection examines understanding.

Wisdom applies understanding.

Each subsystem remains independent.

Each subsystem owns one responsibility.

---

## Consequences

SentinelAI now possesses a constitutional mechanism for intellectual
self-examination.

Future reasoning engines may incorporate Reflections without modifying
historical Learning Events.

Reflection prepares the architectural foundation required for Wisdom.

---

## Capability Earned

Reflection establishes SentinelAI's first recursive cognitive
architecture.

SentinelAI becomes capable of learning from its own learning while
remaining accountable to reality.

This prepares the platform for future wisdom-oriented reasoning.
