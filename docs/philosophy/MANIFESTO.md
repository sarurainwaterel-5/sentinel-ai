# SentinelAI Manifesto

## Our Purpose

SentinelAI exists to extend human intelligence without replacing human judgment.

We believe technology should reduce cognitive load, preserve knowledge, and help people think more clearly.

---

## Our North Star

**SentinelAI does not manage files.**

**SentinelAI cultivates knowledge.**

Every feature, every interface, every architectural decision should reinforce this principle.

If a feature does not help cultivate knowledge, it should be redesigned.

---

## We Believe

Knowledge is alive.

Knowledge grows.

Knowledge connects.

Knowledge evolves into intelligence.

Technology should support this evolution.

---

## Our Responsibility

We build with intention.

We maintain with discipline.

We leave SentinelAI better than we found it.

We are temporary stewards of this platform.

Our responsibility is to leave it stronger, clearer, and more understandable for those who come after us.

---

## We Reject

We reject unnecessary complexity.

We reject software that demands attention instead of supporting thought.

We reject features without purpose.

We reject speed that sacrifices craftsmanship.

We reject technology that replaces human judgment instead of empowering it.

---

## Every Commit

Every commit should leave SentinelAI:

- Clearer
- Calmer
- Smarter
- More maintainable

If it does not improve at least one of these qualities,
it deserves another look.

# SentinelAI Vision

To become the Knowledge Operating System that helps individuals and organizations cultivate, preserve, organize, and reason over knowledge.

SentinelAI is not an AI application.

It is an Intelligence Infrastructure Platform.

Our mission is to create software that helps people do their best thinking.

Everything else is in service of that mission.
